2880 Cells were included in the analysis, with a total species richness of xx. Elevation did not have a consistent effect on the community assembly of different plant traits (Figure 1). Elevation shifted the assembly of plant height and LDMC from environmental filtering at the low elevation, to limiting similarity at the high elevation. Environmental filtering was the dominant assembly mechanism at all elevations for leaf area and SLA, although the filtering effect of SLA was weakest at the mid elevation.
